I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics and
Statistics at the University of New Mexico. My interests include
numerical analysis, scientific computing, and teaching mathematics.

My research focuses on numerical methods for partial differential
equations, particularly domain decomposition methods. I earned my
Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences at the New Jersey Institute of
Technology in 2023.

At UNM, I have taught Calculus III, Applied Ordinary Differential
Equations, and Introduction to Numerical Computing. I enjoy helping
students understand how mathematical methods work and how to use
them in practice.
