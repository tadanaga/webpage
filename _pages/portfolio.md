My research interests include numerical analysis, scientific
computing, and numerical methods for partial differential
equations, particularly domain decomposition methods.

## Domain decomposition methods

Domain decomposition methods divide a large computational
problem into smaller problems that exchange information across
their interfaces.

My work on the Helmholtz equation focuses on finite element
domain decomposition methods for exterior scattering problems.
This includes transmission conditions between subdomains,
the treatment of cross-points where several subdomains meet,
and techniques for truncating unbounded domains.

My doctoral research also included domain decomposition methods
for the fully nonlinear Monge–Ampère equation.
